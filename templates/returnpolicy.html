Return Policy

Antique Watch Shop - Returns and Exchanges

Introduction

Thank you for shopping at Antique Watch Shop! We strive to ensure that you are completely satisfied with your purchase. However, we understand that sometimes things may not work out. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, please read our return policy below.

Eligibility for Returns

Our return policy allows you to return most items within 30 days of receipt. The following conditions apply:

  • The item must be in its original condition, unused, and with all original packaging.
  • The item must be accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase.
  • Items marked as "final sale" or "vintage" are non-returnable unless defective.

How to Return an Item

If you wish to return an item, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service team at support@antiquewatchshop.com or call +1 (123) 456-7890 to request a return authorization.
  2. Once you receive a return authorization, pack the item securely with its original packaging and send it back to the address provided.
  3. We recommend using a traceable shipping service for returns, as we are not responsible for lost or damaged returns during transit.

Refunds and Exchanges

After receiving and inspecting the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed as follows:

  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method used for the purchase.
  • Refunds may take 5-7 business days to process depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • If you choose to exchange the item, we will ship the new item to you at no additional charge, subject to stock availability.

Non-Returnable Items

Some items are not eligible for return, including but not limited to:

  • Gift cards
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items that are not in their original condition or have been damaged by the customer.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately so that we can resolve the issue. We may ask for photos of the damage to expedite the return process.

Shipping Fees

Shipping fees for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect item. If you are returning an item due to an error on our part, we will cover the return shipping costs.
