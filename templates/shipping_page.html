Order Shipping Process

Order Status

  • Order Placed: Your order has been successfully placed, and we're preparing it for shipment.
  • Processing Order: We're processing your order and confirming the availability of your antique watch.
  • Quality Check: Your antique watch is being inspected for authenticity and condition.
  • Dispatched: The watch has been dispatched and is on its way to the shipping address.
  • Shipped: Your watch has been shipped and is in transit to your location.
  • Out for Delivery: Your watch is on its final journey and will be delivered soon.
  • Delivered: Your antique watch has been delivered to the address.
